Questions

Good to know

Do my guests need to download an app?

No. They open the phone camera, scan the QR on the table and the menu opens in their browser. It works on Android and iPhone.

What do I need at the restaurant?

A Windows 10 or 11 computer (or laptop) at the counter with internet. Keep the app open and you'll hear every new order. Your staff can also open the order board on their phones.

Do guests need to join my Wi-Fi?

No. Your menu is hosted online, so it opens on mobile data too.

What happens after the 30-day free trial?

The app asks for a license key. Buy the lifetime or yearly plan here and enter the key. Your menu, tables and QR codes stay exactly as they were, so there's nothing to set up again.

Can I change my menu and prices any time?

Yes. Edit dishes, prices and photos, or mark something sold out. Guests see the change straight away and your QR codes stay the same.

Do you work with restaurants near me?

We work with restaurants, cafés and cloud kitchens across Navi Mumbai, Thane and Mumbai, and with restaurants anywhere in India over WhatsApp and video call.

Can you set it up for me?

Yes. Send us your menu and we'll add the dishes and photos and help you print the QR cards. Message us on WhatsApp.
